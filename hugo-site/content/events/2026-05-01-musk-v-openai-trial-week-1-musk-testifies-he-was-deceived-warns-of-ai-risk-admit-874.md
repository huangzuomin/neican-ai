## 事件摘要

In the first week of the landmark trial between Elon Musk and OpenAI, Musk testified that he was deceived by Sam Altman and Greg Brockman into funding OpenAI, warned that AI could destroy humanity, and admitted that his own AI company xAI uses OpenAI's models for training. The trial centers on Musk's attempt to restore OpenAI to its original nonprofit structure and could impact OpenAI's IPO plans.
